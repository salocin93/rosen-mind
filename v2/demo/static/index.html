Hi, I'm RosenMind.

A tiny language model trained from scratch by Nicolas Rosen on a single gaming GPU. I write fluent English, but I'm small, so I often get facts wrong. Ask me anything and see what happens.

RosenMind is a small experiment and makes things up. Don't rely on its answers.

About RosenMind

RosenMind is a language model built from scratch by Nicolas Rosen: its own tokenizer, pretraining and chat fine-tuning, all on one NVIDIA RTX 4060 Ti. It's thousands of times smaller than the assistants you may know, which is why it's fast and fluent but unreliable.
